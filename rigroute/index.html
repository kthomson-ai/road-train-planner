        Before you drive

        RigRoute is a planning aid. It uses Main Roads WA network and live data, but data can be incomplete or out of date.

        • Road signs, your permit or notice, and your own judgement always come first.
        • Check height, width, length and mass for your actual vehicle and load. RigRoute can't see your truck.
        • Permit, curfew and fatigue information is a guide, not legal advice or a work diary.
        • Business listings (fuel, food, repairs) come from Google and may not suit heavy vehicles.
        • Set the route before you drive, and only report hazards when it's safe.

        Field-test version. Your reports and feedback are shared with the RigRoute team and other testers.
